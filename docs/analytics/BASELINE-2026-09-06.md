**Only 7 of 125 queries sit in positions 4–20.** That matters for reading any
"optimise the striking-distance pages" advice, including the brief this work was
done against: the technique assumes a body of queries in that band, and this
site does not have one yet. 90% of queries are past position 40, where no title
or description change moves them.

The site was password-protected until late August, so this is roughly ten days
of a domain being visible at all.

## Engagement

**No baseline exists.** The site had no analytics of its own before this work,
and Shopify's built-in reports do not break down guide-level engagement. The
events in `EVENTS.md` started publishing on 6 September 2026, and they need a
custom pixel subscribed to them before any of it is collected anywhere. The
first genuine engagement numbers will therefore be *after* figures with nothing
to compare against, and that should be stated plainly rather than papered over
with an invented before.

## Indexing

At the last full check, 43 URLs in the sitemap, all returning 200,
self-canonical and indexable. Coverage state per URL moves daily and is recorded
by `scripts/index-coverage.py`, which labels anything it cannot confirm as
`UNMEASURED` rather than reporting a degraded API response as fact.

## How to re-measure

```
python3 scripts/gsc-page-intent.py --days 28     # queries vs what each page says
python3 scripts/index-coverage.py                # per-URL coverage, retried
python3 scripts/validate-sitemap.py              # every sitemap URL resolves
node scripts/audit-performance.js <url>          # LCP / CLS
```
