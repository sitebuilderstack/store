The problem it addresses

Website projects tend to fail at one of three points, and the three failures need different work.

Some never launch properly. The build stalls, or it ships without the checks that would have caught the broken canonical, the missing alt text, the form that silently drops submissions. Some launch and are never found: technically sound, indexed slowly or not at all, ranking for nothing anyone searches. And some get traffic that does not turn into anything, where the honest answer to “why” requires evidence nobody has collected.

Buying one product solves one of those. Most sites eventually meet all three, in that order, and the systems are written to hand off to each other rather than to overlap.

What using all three looks like

A concrete sequence, in the order the products are meant to be used:

  1. Build. The master build prompt establishes the repository, the CLAUDE.md and the checks. You finish with a site that has never been deployed without its own tests running.
  2. Launch. The launch checklists cover the things that are cheap before launch and expensive after: canonicals, robots, redirects, analytics that actually fires, a 404 that works.
  3. Audit. The SEO toolkit runs in dependency order. Crawlability first, because optimising a title on a URL about to be canonicalised away is wasted work.
  4. Rank. Search Console work on the pages that already have impressions, rather than publishing more pages nobody has found yet.
  5. Convert. The master CRO audit produces findings with evidence and an honesty label — proven bug, strong heuristic, experiment opportunity, or insufficient data — ranked by impact and confidence over effort.
  6. Validate. The experimentation system decides whether you have the traffic to test at all, and says so plainly when you do not.

Who this is for

People who own a website end to end and expect to keep owning it: developers, technical founders, and agencies running the same lifecycle across several sites. It assumes you can read a diff, run a command, and reach your own live site.

Who this is not for

If you only have one of the three problems, buy the one product. The bundle is cheaper than three but more expensive than one, and there is no advantage to owning a system you are not going to open.

It is also not for anyone wanting conversion tactics that manipulate. The conversion toolkit prohibits fake countdowns, fake scarcity, fabricated customer numbers, confirmshaming and the rest by name, and refuses to generate them.

And it is not a service. Nobody audits your site for you; these are systems you run.
