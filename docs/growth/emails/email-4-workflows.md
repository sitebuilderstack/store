# Email 4 — day 6

**Subject:** Which of these are you building?
**Preheader:** Five paths. Pick one; the rest is the same discipline.

---

Most of what I have sent you is general. This one is not — the work looks different
depending on what you are actually building, and the differences are worth naming.

**Building a new site from scratch.** The order is the whole game: research, plan,
architect, build, optimize, secure, test, deploy. Each stage produces something the next
one can be checked against. The trap is starting at "build".
→ [The complete website workflow](https://sitebuilderstack.com/blogs/guides/how-to-build-a-website-with-claude-code)

**Building a Shopify store.** You are writing a theme inside someone else's rendering
pipeline, against a catalogue in someone else's database. The platform has rules an
assistant cannot infer from your repository, and every one of them costs an hour the first
time.
→ [Building a Shopify store with Claude Code](https://sitebuilderstack.com/blogs/guides/build-shopify-store-with-claude-code)

**Building an application.** Higher stakes, so the security and testing sections of your
`CLAUDE.md` earn their length and the SEO section shrinks to nothing. If it is
multi-tenant, one rule matters more than everything else: every query filters by
organisation, and there is a test proving a user from A cannot read B's row.
→ [The SaaS example](https://sitebuilderstack.com/blogs/guides/claude-md-examples-web-development)

**Improving a site that already exists.** Different job. You are not asking "is it ready?"
but "what is wrong, and which of it matters?" Observe before you read the source — reading
the templates first tells you what the site is *supposed* to do, which makes it much harder
to see what it does.
→ [The website audit method](https://sitebuilderstack.com/blogs/guides/claude-code-website-audit)

**Trying to rank.** Half of SEO is verifiable and an assistant is excellent at it. The
other half needs external data, and asked for it, it will invent confident numbers. Keep it
firmly on the first half.
→ [The SEO workflow](https://sitebuilderstack.com/blogs/guides/claude-code-seo-website-optimization)

All five are free to read in full. If one of them answers your question and you never buy
anything, that is a fine outcome.

One more email, then I stop.
