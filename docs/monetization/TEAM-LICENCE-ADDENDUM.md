## Team Licence Addendum

**To:** the Product Licence for **[PRODUCT NAME], Version [X.Y]**, published by
Site Builder Stack ("the Publisher").

**Between:** the Publisher and **[ORGANISATION]** ("the Licensee").

**Order:** [ORDER NUMBER] · **Date:** [DATE] · **Seats:** [N] named users

### 1. What this addendum does

The Product Licence distributed with the product is a single-user licence:
section 5 of that document provides that where more than one person at an
organisation needs to use the files directly, each needs their own licence.

This addendum grants the Licensee **[N] named-user seats** under that same
licence, for the product named above. Every other term of the Product Licence —
permitted use, restrictions, ownership, updates, warranty, liability — applies
unchanged to every seat.

Where this addendum and the Product Licence conflict, this addendum governs,
and only on the subject of how many people may use the product.

### 2. Seats

**2.1** A seat is one named individual. The Licensee names them at purchase and
tells the Publisher in writing when a named individual is replaced.

**2.2** A seat is not a floating pool, a shared login, or a device count. Two
people do not share a seat by using it at different times.

**2.3** Seats cover **people, not websites**. A single seat already permits
unlimited projects, including paid client work, under section 3 of the Product
Licence. Buying seats does not extend or restrict that.

**2.4** A seat holder has the same rights as an individual licensee: use on
unlimited projects, modification, client work, and ownership of everything they
produce.

**2.5** A leaver's seat may be reassigned to a replacement individual. The
leaver must delete their copies of the files. The Licensee is responsible for
that happening.

**2.6** Additional seats are purchased by a further order at the price then
quoted. There is no automatic top-up and no self-service seat management.

### 3. What this addendum does not grant

**3.1** No redistribution. The files may not be posted to a company-wide
intranet, shared drive, wiki, chat workspace, or repository that people without
a seat can read.

**3.2** No resale, sublicensing, rental, or white-labelling of the product.

**3.3** No right to supply the bundle to the Licensee's own clients as a
standing resource. Section 3.6 of the Product Licence (adapted material inside
a specific client deliverable) continues to apply and is not widened.

**3.4** No support, training, onboarding, or consulting commitment.

**3.5** No access to other products. This addendum names one product or bundle.

### 4. Delivery and records

**4.1** The Publisher supplies the same download as the individual product.

**4.2** The Publisher keeps a record of the Licensee, the order, the seat count
and the named individuals, for licence administration only. It is not used for
marketing and is not shared.

**4.3** Where the Licensee requires its own record, this signed addendum is it.

### 5. Term

Perpetual for the version purchased, on the same basis as the Product Licence,
including updates within the same major version where issued.

### 6. Existing licences are unaffected

Nothing in this addendum reduces, restricts, or alters any licence any
individual already holds, whether bought before or after this addendum.

### Notes for the owner (not part of the addendum)

- **Pricing is not in this document** on purpose: the quote carries it, so the
  addendum does not need reissuing when pricing changes.
- The baseline used on the public page is *single-user price × seats*, with no
  discount asserted. Any discount is a decision to make when quoting.
- Section 2.5 (reassignment) and 2.6 (top-ups) are the two clauses most likely
  to be negotiated by a larger buyer.
- This has not been reviewed by a lawyer. It is a plain-language addendum in
  the same register as the existing licence, which carries the same caveat.
