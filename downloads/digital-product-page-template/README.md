# Digital product page template

Two files for writing and checking a Shopify product page that sells a
downloadable item.

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `digital-product-page-copy-template.md` | The copy-and-layout template. Twelve sections, each with a purpose, a shape and a fill-in. |
| `digital-product-page-review-checklist.md` | The tick-list to run before calling a page finished, and again after any theme change. Includes three one-line shell checks. |

**This is a copy-and-layout template, not software.** There is no Shopify theme,
app or Liquid section here. Nothing installs and nothing is generated. You write
the prose and build the sections with whatever your theme already gives you.

Free to use and adapt. From [SiteBuilderStack](https://sitebuilderstack.com).

---

## Start here

1. Open the template and work down it, replacing everything in
   `[square brackets]`.
2. Delete any section your product genuinely does not need. An empty section is
   worse than no section.
3. Run the checklist against the result.

Two rules decide whether it works:

- **Every claim must be checkable by someone who has not bought the product.**
  If you cannot point at where a number came from, cut it.
- **Requirements go before benefits.** A buyer who finds out the prerequisite
  after paying is a support ticket at best.

The section most pages skip is number 5, the genuine preview. Most digital
product pages answer "how much is there" — a folder tree, a file count — and
never answer "is it any good". Only the second question is being asked at the
moment somebody decides.
