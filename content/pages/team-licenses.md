## What a team licence is for

Every product here ships under a **single-user licence**. One person, unlimited
projects, including paid client work. That licence is deliberately generous
about *projects* and deliberately strict about *people*: the files may not be
shared across a team, an agency, or an organisation.

A team licence changes one thing — how many named people may hold and use the
files directly. Nothing else about the licence changes.

## The distinction that matters

Most enquiries about this turn out to be one of the two things below. They are
not the same:

| Situation | What you need |
| --- | --- |
| **One person** runs the workflows and builds sites with them, including for paying clients — any number of clients, any number of sites | The single-user licence you already have. Nothing further to buy. |
| **Several people** at your organisation each open the files, run the prompts, and adapt the templates | A team licence with a seat for each of them |

Put the other way round:

- **Seats are people, not websites.** Three seats do not mean three client
  sites. One seat already covers unlimited sites.
- **Client work is already allowed** on a single-user licence, and material you
  adapt for a specific client deliverable may go to that client as part of the
  work product.
- **A team licence is not a redistribution licence.** It does not permit
  resale, public posting, repackaging, or handing the bundle to clients as a
  standing resource.
- **Seats are named.** A licence is per person, not a floating pool, and not a
  shared login.

## Seats and what they cover

A team licence covers **one product or bundle** — the one you choose — for the
number of named users you buy. It is a one-time purchase, like the individual
licence, with the same access to same-major-version updates.

## Pricing

**Team pricing is quoted on request, not published here.** What we can tell you
before you ask is the baseline it starts from: the published single-user price
for the product you want, multiplied by the number of seats. Nothing on this
page claims a discount, because no multi-seat price has been set yet — quoting
a saving before there is a price to save against would be inventing one.

If you tell us the product and the seat count, you get a written quote with the
terms attached, and you decide from there.

## How a team licence is delivered

1. You send the request below: product or bundle, number of seats, who to
   invoice.
2. We reply with a quote and the team terms — a short addendum that applies to
   the team purchase only and changes nothing about any licence you already
   hold.
3. On acceptance we raise the invoice, and on payment you receive the download
   and a named-user record for your file.
4. Adding a seat later is a follow-up request; there is no self-service seat
   portal, and this page does not pretend there is one.

## What a team licence does not include

- No resale, sublicensing, or redistribution rights
- No training, onboarding, or consulting hours
- No access to unreleased products
- No support commitment beyond the ordinary one on the [contact page](/pages/contact)
- No change to the [product licence](/pages/licence) you hold today

If what you actually want is an outside pair of eyes on one site rather than
files for your team, the [Website Launch & Conversion
Review](/pages/website-review) is the other request to make.
