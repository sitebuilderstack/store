Every guide on this site belongs to a track, and every track has an order that
was chosen for a reason. This page shows how far through each one you are.

## How it remembers

In your browser, and nowhere else.

Marking a guide complete writes a single entry to `localStorage` under one key.
There is no account, no email address, no identifier, and nothing is
transmitted — not to this site, not to an analytics service. The site records
that *a* guide was marked complete, never which browser marked it.

The honest trade is worth stating plainly: because it is stored locally, it does
not follow you to another device or another browser, and clearing site data
clears it. That is the cost of not asking you to create an account, and it seemed
the better side of the bargain for something that is a reading list rather than a
qualification.

If you are in a private window, or your browser blocks site data, the controls
do not appear at all. A button that pretends to remember and then forgets is
worse than no button.

## What "complete" means

Whatever you want it to mean. Nothing checks up on you, and there is no
certificate at the end.

A guide is marked complete for the whole site rather than per track, so a guide
that appears in two tracks is ticked in both once you have read it. Reading it
once is reading it once.

## Saved for later

Separate from completion, and deliberately so. "Save for later" is for the guide
you want to come back to; "Mark complete" is for the one you have finished.
Conflating them would make both less useful.
