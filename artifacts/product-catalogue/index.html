Before you buy

What every product includes, and what none of them does

How it arrives

  • Instant download after Shopify checkout — a zip of Markdown files, commands and scripts you open in your own Claude Code session
  • One-time purchase; no account subscription, no seat licences
  • Nothing runs on our servers; nothing phones home

What you may do

  • Use it on an unlimited number of your own sites and commercial projects
  • Use it on paid client work and charge what you like
  • Modify, adapt and extend any file; keep everything you produce with it

What you may not do

  • Share, resell, sublicense or upload the files anywhere
  • Republish them as your own toolkit or course
  • Share one licence across a team — it is single-user

What it is not
